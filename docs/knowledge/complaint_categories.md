# GameOps AI - complaint categories

## Bugs and crashes
Crashes, freezes, corrupted saves, broken quests, glitches and errors.

## Performance and optimization
Low or unstable frame rate, stuttering, long loading times, high CPU or GPU usage, memory leaks.

## Servers and connectivity
Server outages, disconnections, high ping, lag, desync and login failures.

## Gameplay balance
Overpowered or underpowered weapons, characters or builds, unfair difficulty, nerfs and buffs.

## Monetization and microtransactions
Pay-to-win concerns, loot boxes, expensive premium stores, battle passes and aggressive purchase prompts.

## Matchmaking
Long queue times, unbalanced teams and rank mismatches.

## User experience
Confusing menus, interface, controls, tutorials and accessibility options.

## Content and updates
Too little content, repetition, missing endgame, unfulfilled roadmaps.

## Customer support
Unanswered tickets, refunds, mistaken bans and account recovery.

## Other
Negative reviews where no specific category was predicted.
